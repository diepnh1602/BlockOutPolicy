# Privacy Policy for Block Out

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Out ("the game", "we", "us") handles information when you play the game or contact us. For questions or privacy requests, email SUPPORT_EMAIL.

## 1. Information handled by the game

**Game data on your device.** The game stores settings, a player name you choose, avatar and frame selections, game progress, and inventory and purchase-entitlement records on your device. You can use a nickname; please do not include sensitive personal information in it. The current game does not require an account or upload your local profile to a developer-operated account service.

**Advertising and technical data.** The game integrates Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. When these services run, they may collect device and app identifiers (including an advertising identifier where available), IP address, device model, operating system, language, approximate location inferred from IP address, app usage, ad impressions and clicks, and technical or diagnostic information. Processing depends on the platform, SDK configuration, permissions, and applicable privacy choices.

**Purchases.** If you make an in-app purchase, the app store and Unity In-App Purchasing process transaction information. The game receives product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase status, and entitlement information to deliver or restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

**Support messages.** If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to provide, such as a description of an issue or a screenshot. Please avoid sending passwords or payment-card information.

## 2. How information is used

Information is used to operate the game, remember your settings and profile, save progress and inventory, deliver and restore purchases, provide and measure advertising, diagnose technical issues, prevent fraud, and answer support or privacy requests.

Where applicable law requires consent for advertising identifiers, personalized advertising, or other non-essential processing, that processing requires your consent. Continuing to play does not by itself constitute consent to such processing. Other processing may be necessary to provide a requested service, comply with legal obligations, or support legitimate interests such as security and responding to support requests, where permitted by law.

## 3. Third-party services and sharing

The game uses the following services, which describe their own processing, retention, and privacy controls in their policies:

- Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and Unity In-App Purchasing: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
- Google Play, for Android distribution and purchases: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Apple App Store, when the game is distributed on Apple devices: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Advertising providers may share information with their advertising and measurement partners as described in their policies. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, prevent fraud, or handle a business transfer with appropriate protections.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising-related transfers may be treated as a "sale", "sharing", or targeted advertising under some privacy laws; any applicable rights and opt-out choices still apply.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or removed. On Android, you can use the system's app storage settings to clear the game's data. On other platforms, removing the game generally removes its local data; operating-system backups or store purchase history may remain. Clearing data may erase progress and consumable inventory, and it does not cancel purchases or subscriptions managed by an app store.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and any related legal or security obligations. Service providers retain information according to their policies and applicable law. Store transaction records may need to be kept for accounting, fraud prevention, or other legal obligations.

To request access to or deletion of information held by us, email SUPPORT_EMAIL with the subject "Block Out privacy request". Describe your request and provide only the information needed to identify the relevant correspondence or transaction. We may ask for reasonable verification and will respond within the time required by applicable law. Requests about data held independently by Unity, Google, or Apple can also be submitted through their privacy channels above.

## 5. Your choices and rights

You can edit your local player name and avatar in the game and manage device permissions, advertising identifiers, and ad-personalization preferences in your device settings. Where an ad provides a privacy or information control, you can use it to view the provider's choices. These controls may limit personalization without removing all ads or technical processing.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising uses. Contact SUPPORT_EMAIL to exercise applicable rights. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 6. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13, or under a higher age where local law requires it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact SUPPORT_EMAIL so we can investigate and delete it where required. Children's data must receive the protections required by applicable law and platform rules.

## 7. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Third-party services may process information outside your country, under the safeguards described in their privacy policies and required by applicable law.

## 8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.

For support, questions, or privacy requests about Block Out, email SUPPORT_EMAIL.
